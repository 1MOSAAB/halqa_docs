Privacy Policy

Halqa · Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information this app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

• Account information: name, phone number, and (optionally) email address you provide when signing up. • Verification data: one-time codes sent to your phone to verify your account or reset your password. • Booking data: the shops, barbers, services, and appointment times you book, cancel, or rate. • Shop/barber data: if you operate a shop or barber account, the business details, services, and schedules you provide. • Photos (optional): a shop's logo and background photo, and a barber's profile photo. They are shown to everyone using the app. We shrink each photo and remove its hidden details (such as where it was taken) before storing it. • Location (optional): if you allow it, your phone's location is used to center the shop map near you and to list the nearest shops first. For the map it stays on your device. For the shops list it is sent to our server with that request only, to sort the shops by distance - it is never stored, logged or linked to your account. • Technical data: basic request metadata (such as IP address) used for security and rate-limiting, to prevent abuse of signup and verification features.

2. How We Use Information

• To create and secure your account, including OTP verification and login protection. • To let you browse shops/barbers and make, manage, or cancel bookings. • To show ratings and reviews to other users. • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only: • With the shop/barber you book with, so they can fulfill your appointment. • With service providers who help us operate the app (for example, SMS providers used to deliver verification codes). • With the map provider (Geoapify), when you open the shop map: your device loads the map images directly from it, so it receives your device's IP address and the area of the map being shown. • With Google's connection check, only when the app can't reach our server: your device briefly contacts it to tell whether the problem is your internet connection or our server. It receives your device's IP address. • When required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the app.

4. Data Retention

We retain account and booking information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the app's services, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

5. Your Choices

You can update your profile information in the app. You can also delete your account directly from the app (Profile > Delete Account) at any time. Deleting your account replaces your name and other personal details on file with a generic placeholder and clears the rest of your personal information; some booking records may still be kept in this de-identified form where needed for the shop's records or our legal/accounting obligations, but they are no longer linked to you by name, phone, or email. A barber's profile photo is deleted along with the account. You can also remove a photo yourself at any time.

6. Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing, and verification codes are hashed and time-limited. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
