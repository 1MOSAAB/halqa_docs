Delete your Halqa account

Halqa · Last updated: November 2, 2026

You can delete your Halqa account and the personal information in it at any time.

In the app

Open Halqa → Profile → Delete account (at the bottom, next to Log out) → enter your password → Delete.

Without the app

Email mosaabemad911@gmail.com or call +20-1515315447 from the phone number on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm it's your account and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your name, phone number, email address and password, any verification codes, and a barber's profile photo. Upcoming bookings are cancelled.

What is kept

Past bookings and the ratings you left stay in the shop's records for record-keeping and legal/accounting reasons. They show as "Deleted user" and are no longer linked to your name, phone number or email.

Barber and shop owner accounts

Barbers can delete their account in the app once they have no shifts left at any shop (the shop owner removes them) and no upcoming bookings. Shop owner accounts can't be deleted in the app: contact us and we'll remove you from your shop first, then delete the account.
